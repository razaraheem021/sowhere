Sowhere privacy policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Sowhere ("the app") is published by Your Name. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

Sowhere does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts and no server.

Information stored on your device

The plants you add, their care history, your reminders, your chosen country, temperature unit and saved plants are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with "Erase my garden" in the app, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the app.

Backups you create

"Copy backup" copies your garden to your device's clipboard as text. Where you paste or send that text is your choice. The app does not send it anywhere.

Permissions

Notifications: used to show plant care reminders that you set up. Notifications are created on your device.

The app also declares technical permissions that show no prompt: internet access (required by the app's web view; the app makes no network requests for its content), restarting scheduled reminders after the device reboots, and scheduling alarms at the time you chose.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking software.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
