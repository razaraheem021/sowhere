Sowhere privacy policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Sowhere ("the app") is published by Raza Raheem. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

Your garden stays on your device. Sowhere has no user accounts and no server of its own, and we do not collect, sell or receive your plants, reminders or other content. The app shows advertising provided by Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to deliver those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The plants you add, their care history, your reminders, your chosen country, temperature unit and saved plants are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time with "Erase my garden" in the app, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the app.

Backups you create

"Copy backup" copies your garden to your device's clipboard as text. Where you paste or send that text is your choice. The app does not send it anywhere.

Advertising

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google automatically collects information from your device, including your IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information such as crash and performance data. This information goes to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Ads may be personalised based on your interests. You can turn off ad personalisation or reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, under Google > Ads. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy choices" on the app's Help screen.

Permissions

Notifications: used to show plant care reminders that you set up. Notifications are created on your device.

The app also declares permissions that show no prompt: internet access and network state (used to load ads), the advertising ID (used by Google AdMob), restarting scheduled reminders after the device reboots, and scheduling alarms at the time you chose.

Analytics and other third parties

Apart from Google AdMob, the app contains no analytics or tracking software, and we do not share information with any other third party.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
